1. Diagnostic Services & Pathological Disclaimer

Laboratory investigations are performed on advanced automated analyzers following certified quality control standards. Diagnostic tests are supportive clinical tools and must always be correlated with clinical symptoms by your treating physician.

2. Home Sample Collection Service

We provide home sample collection across Hadapsar, Mohammad Wadi, Handewadi, Sayyad Nagar, Autadwadi, Magarpatta, and Pune suburbs. Patients must comply with required pre-test fasting instructions (e.g. 8–12 hours for Fasting Glucose or Lipid Profile) for test accuracy.

3. Reporting Turnaround Commitment

Routine investigations (CBC, Blood Sugar, Urine Routine, Thyroid) are generally reported within 4 to 6 hours directly to your WhatsApp. Advanced specialized panels may require 12 to 24 hours. Repeat tests for clinical verification may occasionally require additional processing time.

4. Booking, Rescheduling & Cancellation

Appointments can be booked online, by phone, or on WhatsApp 24×7. You may reschedule or cancel your home collection at zero charge anytime before our phlebotomist arrives at your premises.
