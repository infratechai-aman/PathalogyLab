Patient Confidentiality & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Accurate Pathology Laboratory, Hadapsar, Pune

Medical Privacy Guarantee: Accurate Pathology Laboratory treats all patient health and clinical diagnostic records with strict confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, or trade your medical or contact details to third-party advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

When you book a diagnostic test or home sample collection, we collect:

  • Patient Identity: Full name, age, gender, and contact phone number.
  • Service Location: Doorstep address within Hadapsar, Mohammad Wadi, Handewadi, Sayyad Nagar, Autadwadi, and Pune for phlebotomist dispatch.
  • Prescription & History: Doctor advice slips or requested health screening packages.
  • Clinical Diagnostics: Test outcomes analyzed through automated barcoded laboratory systems.

2. How We Use Patient Data

Collected data is strictly used to:

  • Perform high-precision pathology and biochemical investigations.
  • Transmit verified digital reports directly to the patient's authenticated WhatsApp number and SMS.
  • Dispatch certified phlebotomists safely for sterile home sample collection.
  • Notify patients or their consulting doctors in cases of critical / panic medical values.

3. WhatsApp & Electronic Communication

By scheduling a diagnostic test, you authorize Accurate Pathology Laboratory to send appointment reminders, phlebotomist arrival updates, and signed PDF test reports via WhatsApp and SMS. You can withdraw WhatsApp messaging at any time by contacting our 24×7 helpdesk.

4. Sample Integrity & Barcode Tracking

All blood, serum, and urine samples are collected in sterile, single-use vacuum vacutainer tubes identified with unique machine-readable barcodes at the patient's bedside, preventing specimen mix-ups.
